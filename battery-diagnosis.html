🔋 Battery Diagnosis

Professional Battery Testing & Diagnosis

Complete Battery Diagnosis

We check your battery, starting system and charging system to identify the actual cause of your vehicle's starting problem.

STEP 01

🔍 Vehicle Inspection

We identify the vehicle make, model, year and battery specifications before starting the diagnosis.

STEP 02

👁️ Visual Battery Inspection

Battery casing, terminals, cables, corrosion, leakage and physical damage are carefully inspected.

STEP 03

⚡ Voltage Test

Battery resting voltage and charge level are measured using professional diagnostic equipment.

STEP 04

🔋 Battery Health & CCA Test

Battery health, Cold Cranking Amps (CCA) and starting capacity are tested to determine battery condition.

STEP 05

🚗 Cranking Test

We check voltage drop and battery performance while starting the engine.

STEP 06

⚙️ Alternator Charging Test

Alternator output and charging voltage are checked to make sure the battery is receiving the correct charge.

STEP 07

🔌 Electrical Drain Check

If the battery repeatedly goes flat, we check for abnormal electrical consumption and possible battery drain.

STEP 08

📋 Diagnosis Result

After testing, we clearly explain whether your battery is good, weak, requires replacement, or if there is a charging-system issue.
