Our Diagnosis Process

A structured inspection to identify battery-related problems.

STEP 01

🔍 Vehicle Inspection

We identify the vehicle make, model, year and relevant battery specifications before beginning the diagnosis.

STEP 02

👁️ Visual Battery Inspection

Battery casing, terminals, cables, corrosion, leakage and visible physical damage are carefully inspected.

STEP 03

⚡ Battery Voltage Test

Resting battery voltage and charge level are measured using appropriate diagnostic equipment.

STEP 04

🔋 Battery Health & CCA Test

Battery health, Cold Cranking Amps (CCA) and starting capability are checked to assess battery condition.

STEP 05

🚗 Cranking Test

Starting performance and voltage drop during engine cranking are checked to identify possible battery issues.

STEP 06

⚙️ Alternator Charging Test

Charging voltage and alternator performance are checked to determine whether the battery is receiving appropriate charging.

STEP 07

🔌 Electrical Drain Check

If the battery repeatedly becomes discharged, we can check for abnormal electrical consumption or possible battery drain.

STEP 08

📋 Diagnosis Result

After testing, we explain the findings and advise whether the battery appears healthy, weak, requires replacement, or whether further charging-system inspection may be needed.

Possible Diagnosis Results

🟢 Battery Condition: Good
🟠 Battery Condition: Weak
🔴 Battery Replacement Recommended
🔧 Charging System Issue
